# Lumina LMS — Brand Video

**Runtime:** 2:30–2:40 · **Narration:** ~390 words · **Voice:** English, female

---

## Direction

**Voice.** Warm, measured, unhurried — closer to a documentary narrator than an
ad read. She is explaining something she believes in, not selling it. Smile
through scenes 4–11. Settle and steady from 13 onward, where the film turns
technical. Never rush the last line.

**Music.** Understated and building. Felt piano or soft keys under the open, no
percussion until scene 4. A pulse enters with the catalogue, lifts at scene 12
where the film hands over to the people who build the courses, and is fullest
under 15–16. Resolve — don't crescendo — on the end card. Avoid stock corporate
uplift; this should sound like something made for one company rather than for
any company.

**Grade.** Screens as captured, dark theme throughout except scene 3, which
opens light and transitions to dark on the brand reveal. Motion is slow push-in
and gentle parallax. No swish transitions, no screen-flip 3D, nothing that
draws attention to the edit.

**Pacing note.** To land at exactly 2:30, trim scene 8 to its first sentence and
cut the final sentence of scene 15. Both are marked `[TRIM]`.

## Production notes

**Claims to keep honest.** Everything narrated here is implemented and was
verified running. Two qualifiers worth respecting in any cutdown: single sign-on
is OIDC against a provider you configure, and email requires an SMTP relay —
neither is on by default, and the film should not imply either works untouched
out of the box.

**Sequence that films well.** Shoot scenes 4–11 as one continuous learner
session on a single account rather than as isolated screens; the cuts land
better when the data is consistent across them.

**Screens.** Captured from the running application at 1280×720 — real data, not
mockups. See `marketing/shots/`.
