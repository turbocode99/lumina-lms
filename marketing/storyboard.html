# Act Dur On screen Narration

Production notes

For the voice artist and the edit

The voice here is a placeholder

This is your browser's built-in speech engine. It is useful for judging pacing and copy, and it will not sound human, because no browser engine does.

Two things help now: pick a voice labelled Natural or Online in the list — Edge on Windows has Aria, Jenny and Emma, which are neural and markedly better — and note that the narration is spoken sentence by sentence so it breathes rather than running on.

Getting a human voice

For the real thing, the clean read in brand-video-script.md goes either to a voice artist, or to a neural service — ElevenLabs, Azure Neural, Google WaveNet — which is what most product films of this kind actually use.

Record scene by scene against the shot list below and the durations stay honest.

Direction for the read

Warm, measured, unhurried — closer to a documentary narrator than an ad read. She is walking someone through a system she knows well.

Lift slightly at Act III where Daniel signs in. Settle and steady through Act V, where the film answers to the auditor rather than the learner.

Music

The bed playing here is generated in the page, not a licensed track — again, for pacing only. Warm, unhurried, building through Act IV and resolving rather than crescendoing on the end card.

Avoid stock corporate uplift: this should sound made for one company, not for any company.

One person, all the way through

The film follows two real accounts from the seeded data — Priya administers, Daniel learns — and the footage shows those same people throughout.

Keep that continuity in the edit. The moment the names stop matching the screens, it reads as a feature list again.

Keep it honest

Everything narrated is implemented and was verified running. Two qualifiers to respect: single sign-on is OIDC against a provider you configure, and email needs an SMTP relay.

Neither is on by default, so the film must not imply either works untouched out of the box.

The rendered cut

This page is the storyboard. The film itself is rendered to marketing/lumina-walkthrough-no-vo.mp4 — 4:05 at 1920×1080, with the motion, crossfades, burned-in titles and music bed baked in.

Only the narration is outstanding there, and it wants a neural voice rather than a browser one.

Still to film
